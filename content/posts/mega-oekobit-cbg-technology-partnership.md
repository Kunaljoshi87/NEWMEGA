MEGA Engineering has signed a technology partnership agreement with **OEKOBIT, Germany**, to develop turnkey compressed biogas (CBG) projects across **India, South East Asia and Africa**.

## Who does what

OEKOBIT licenses its substrate-flexible biogas plant technology and supports process design. MEGA engineers, procures, builds, commissions and supports the plant on site, so clients have one EPC contract and one project manager.

| Partner | Role |
|---|---|
| OEKOBIT, Germany | Technology licence, process know-how, digestion concept and key plant technology |
| MEGA Engineering, India | Detailed engineering, procurement, in-house manufacturing, construction, commissioning, operator training and after-sales support |

## What the plants deliver

- CBG with **≥ 97% methane**, meeting IS 16087, at 250 bar
- **97–98% methane recovery** with MEGA's two-stage membrane upgrading
- Organic fertiliser from digestate, and optional CO₂ recovery from the upgrading off-gas

OEKOBIT reports more than 280 biogas and biomethane projects worldwide. MEGA has delivered 100+ projects in 15+ countries since 2010.

## Who it is for

Sugar mills and distilleries, agri-business, dairy and livestock farms, food, starch and palm oil processors, and municipal and organic-waste developers.
