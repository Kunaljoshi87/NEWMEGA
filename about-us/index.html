Who we are

Built on Experience. Driven by Progress.

Headquartered in Pune, India

MEGA Engineering is an independent engineering and manufacturing organization based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. The company brings together its people, facilities and organizational expertise under one roof, forming the foundation of the MEGA organization.

MEGA Engineering commenced operations in 2010, initially focusing on distillery engineering and industrial process applications. The company established its foundation through the development of process engineering expertise and industrial project experience.

As the organisation evolved, its business scope expanded to encompass integrated process plants, specialised process systems, industrial equipment manufacturing and engineering services. This development enabled MEGA to broaden its presence across multiple industrial sectors and address increasingly diverse process requirements.

Today, MEGA's portfolio spans fuel ethanol and distillation, multiple-effect evaporation and drying, industrial wastewater treatment and Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD), BioCNG systems, carbon dioxide recovery, solvent recovery and custom-engineered process equipment.

Through this evolution, MEGA has continued to develop its engineering, manufacturing and project execution capabilities, supported by its people, infrastructure and accumulated industry experience.
