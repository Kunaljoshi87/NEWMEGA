Who we are

We know. We design. We build.

Headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra

Engineering, EPC delivery and process equipment capabilities serving Indian and international industrial projects.

Mega Engineering began in 2010 with a focus on distillery engineering and industrial process solutions. The company’s service offering expanded to include complete plants, specialty process systems, equipment manufacturing, and engineering services for multiple industrial sectors.

Our approach connects process design, equipment engineering, project coordination, fabrication, site installation support and commissioning. We focus on practical, application-specific solutions: engineering must reflect plant capacity, raw material characteristics, utilities, product specifications and project constraints.

Today our principal areas include fuel ethanol and distillation, multiple-effect evaporation and drying, wastewater treatment and zero liquid discharge, BioCNG systems, carbon dioxide recovery, solvent recovery, and custom-engineered process equipment.

About Mega Engineering

Clear answers for customers, procurement teams and engineering partners.

What does Mega Engineering do?

Mega Engineering delivers EPC, process engineering and equipment-related solutions for industrial plants, including fuel ethanol, distillery, BioCNG, CO₂ recovery, evaporation and zero liquid discharge applications.

Where is Mega Engineering based?

Mega Engineering is headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, India. Its existing website also identifies manufacturing facilities in the Talegaon Dabhade area.

Does Mega Engineering provide complete EPC services?

Its service offering includes engineering, procurement and construction-related project delivery, alongside individual process packages and custom equipment. Exact scope depends on the project contract.

Which industries does Mega Engineering serve?

Its published business lines include distillery and ethanol, biofuels, chemicals, food and beverage, industrial process equipment and environmental treatment systems.
