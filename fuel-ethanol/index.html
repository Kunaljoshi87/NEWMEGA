Fuel Ethanol Plants

End-to-end EPC for fuel ethanol plants: process engineering, equipment supply, erection and commissioning. 100+ projects delivered worldwide.

Fuel Ethanol Plants

MEGA Engineering designs, manufactures, erects and commissions complete fuel ethanol and ENA plants, from feedstock preparation to dehydrated ethanol, with the utilities and effluent systems around them. Every plant is engineered by our own team and its key equipment is fabricated in our works near Pune.

What We Deliver

  • Feedstock preparation: cane juice and molasses handling, grain milling and liquefaction
  • Fermentation: continuous and batch fermentation, including high-gravity fermentation
  • Distillation: ENA and rectified spirit distillation, heat-integrated for low steam use
  • Dehydration: molecular sieve dehydration to fuel-grade ethanol
  • Spent wash treatment: biogas, multiple effect evaporation, spray drying and DDGS dryers
  • By-products and utilities: CO₂ recovery, condensate polishing, cooling and storage

Proprietary Technology

Our own fermentation, distillation and dehydration technologies (MEGA-Swift, Continuo, Eficiente, Pure+ and PSA) are designed for local feedstock and conditions, so each plant is matched to the raw material and the product the client needs.

Proven from 5 KLPD to 300 KLPD

MEGA distilleries run on cane juice, molasses and grain in India, Africa, the Americas and Asia, including repeat orders from existing clients. View our project references →

Frequently Asked Questions

Which feedstocks can a MEGA ethanol plant use?

Cane juice, B and C molasses, and grains such as corn, rice and wheat. Plants can also be designed to run on more than one feedstock.

What capacities do you build?

Distilleries from 5 KLPD to 300 KLPD. See our project references for plants delivered and under execution.

Can you also handle spent wash and CO₂?

Yes. We supply biogas, evaporation, spray drying and condensate polishing for zero liquid discharge, and CO₂ recovery plants for fermentation gas.
