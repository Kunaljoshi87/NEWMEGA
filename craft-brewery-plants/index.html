Craft Brewery Plants

Custom craft brewery plants for microbreweries and brewpubs. Mega Engineering designs efficient brewhouses for consistent, high-quality beer.

Craft brewery Plants

We at Mega Engineering, manufacture and supply Brewery Equipment, Large Scale Industrial Breweries, Micro Brewery Equipment, Mini Brewery and Pub Breweries. Our professional team ensure manufacturing of excellent equipment, which help our clients to enhance their business activities.

We supply Brewery Plants which has world class manufacturing capability to achieve excellent and consistent product quality at lower cost. All the brewery tanks built by Mega are designed to international codes and standards with high quality surface finish. Microbreweries can be installed in existing restaurants, Pubs, Hotels or malls. We can design the brewery plants as per space available and as per buyer’s equipment. Approximately space requirement for microbrewery plant is about 400 sq. feet. We are the experts in microbrewery manufacturing in India (Excluding milling, raw materials, storage, kegging)
