01 · Vinasse

A Distillery's Nightmare, and a Valuable Waste

Every litre of ethanol made from molasses leaves behind roughly 8 to 15 litres of vinasse, also called spent wash. It is dark brown, acidic, foul-smelling when it stands, and one of the strongest industrial effluents there is. Yet almost everything in it can be recovered: the organics as biogas, the potassium as fertiliser, the solids as cattle feed, and the water for reuse.

Why It Is a Nightmare

  • Volume: many times the ethanol produced, every day the distillery runs
  • Strength: COD of 80,000–120,000 mg/L and BOD of 40,000–60,000 mg/L
  • Acidity and colour: pH 3.5–4.5 and a persistent dark brown colour
  • Salts: heavy loads of potassium, chlorides and sulphates
  • Odour: turns foul if it stands in lagoons
  • Regulation: zero liquid discharge is now the norm for molasses distilleries

You must treat it.

Raw vs Bio-Methanated Vinasse

Anaerobic digestion turns most of the organic load into biogas and neutralises the liquid. The salts, and with them the potash value, stay in the bio-methanated spent wash (BMSW).

03 · Routes to zero liquid discharge

Two Ways to Close the Loop on Vinasse

MEGA delivers both routes to ZLD. The biogas route recovers energy first, then concentrates the bio-methanated spent wash (BMSW) into cattle-feed concentrate or a dry powder. The incineration route concentrates raw vinasse to 55–60 Brix and burns it. In both, the evaporator condensate is polished and recycled. The spray dryer in this catalogue is the last step of the biogas route.

04 · Route 1 · Biogas · step 1

MEGA-CSTR Vinasse Biogas Plant

A continuous stirred tank reactor digests raw vinasse anaerobically, cutting COD by about two-thirds and producing methane-rich biogas. Central and lateral agitators keep the biomass in suspension; a degasser and lamella clarifier return active sludge to the reactor.

05 · Route 1 · Biogas · step 2

BMSW Degassing & Multiple Effect Evaporation

After biomethanation the spent wash carries dissolved CO₂, H₂S and ammonia, at pH 7.5–8.5. Heated directly, these gases flash off and foam the evaporator. MEGA's vacuum degasser removes them first, without steam sparging or antifoam, so the evaporators run clean and the condensate stays clear.

Condensate Stripping

Raw process condensate leaves the evaporator at pH 8.0–9.2, with COD up to 4,000 ppm (mostly ammoniacal nitrogen) and TDS up to 1,500 ppm (mostly VFAs). MEGA's fractional stripping column, heat-integrated with the evaporator, removes these at source and cuts the load on the condensate polishing unit.

  • Crystal clear process condensate
  • No antifoam
  • Low downtime, 24 × 7 operation
  • Standby calandria
  • Less electricity, operator friendly
06 · The MEGA spray dryer · process

From 5–6% Liquid to 95% Powder in Five Stages

A multiple effect evaporator removes the cheap water first. The spray dryer then takes the concentrate to a dry powder using hot air from a bagasse or biogas-fired generator.

08 · Hot air generator

Bagasse / Biogas-Fired Hot Air Generator

The dryer runs on the distillery's own bagasse or biogas. A two-compartment furnace with a pulsating grate burns the fuel, an air heater recovers heat into the combustion air, and a dilution blower delivers hot air at about 250 °C to the spray chamber.

09 · Route 2 · Incineration

Raw Vinasse Evaporation with Condensate Polishing

On the incineration route, raw vinasse goes straight to multiple effect evaporation and is concentrated to 55–60 Brix, strong enough to fire in an incineration boiler. The evaporated water does not go to waste: process condensate is treated in a condensate polishing unit (CPU) and recycled to the process.

When the Incineration Route Fits

  • The distillery wants to use vinasse directly as boiler fuel
  • Steam demand is high and an incineration boiler is planned or in place
  • Water recovery matters: the CPU returns evaporator condensate to the process

Weigh against: an incineration boiler carries high capital and maintenance cost.

MEGA Scope on This Route

  • Raw vinasse multiple effect evaporation to 55–60 Brix
  • Condensate polishing unit for evaporator condensate
  • Maximum heat integration for the highest steam economy
  • CIP provision for all evaporators
  • PLC-SCADA control with VFDs to save power
