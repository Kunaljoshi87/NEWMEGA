Project Management Services

On-time, on-budget plant delivery. Mega Engineering provides EPC project management, scheduling, procurement and site execution services.

Project management services

MEGA Engineering manages process plant projects from kick-off to handover, with one team responsible for schedule, cost, quality and safety. Our project managers work alongside our own engineering and manufacturing teams, so decisions are made quickly and interfaces stay under control.

What We Manage

  • Planning and scheduling: project execution plan, milestone schedule and progress tracking
  • Engineering coordination: deliverables, approvals and interfaces between disciplines
  • Procurement and expediting: vendor selection, purchase orders, follow-up and logistics
  • Quality and inspection: project-specific quality plans, NDT and third-party inspection
  • Construction management: erection supervision, site safety and subcontractor coordination
  • Commissioning and handover: pre-commissioning, start-up, performance runs and documentation
  • Reporting: regular progress reports and review meetings with the client team

How We Work

  1. Kick-offScope, schedule, responsibilities and communication plan agreed with the client.
  2. Engineering and procurementDeliverables and orders tracked against the milestone schedule.
  3. ManufacturingEquipment and skids fabricated and inspected in our own works.
  4. Site executionErection supervised by our engineers, with safety and quality checks.
  5. CommissioningStart-up, performance demonstration and handover to the operating team.

Why It Matters

With engineering, manufacturing and site execution under one roof, MEGA gives you a single point of contact and single-point responsibility for the whole plant.
