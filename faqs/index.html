FAQ's

Answers to common questions about MEGA Engineering, our plants and how we work. Cannot find what you need? Contact our engineers.

What kind of plants does MEGA Engineering build?

Distillery and fuel ethanol plants, CO₂ recovery plants, biogas and Bio-CNG plants, evaporation and drying plants including vinasse spray dryers, zero liquid discharge systems, modular process skids and critical process equipment.

Do you supply complete turnkey plants?

Yes. We cover technology, engineering, manufacturing, erection, commissioning and after-sales service, as a single partner with one point of contact.

What capacity range do you cover for distilleries?

From 5 KLPD to 300 KLPD, on cane juice, molasses and grain.

Do you manufacture the equipment yourselves?

Yes. Our works at Talegaon Dabhade, near Pune, has more than 2,500 tonnes per year of fabrication capacity and is 2 km from ICD Talegaon, with road links to JNPT port for export.

Which certifications do you hold?

MEGA is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 by TÜV NORD. Third-party inspection by TÜV, Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or DNV is available on request.

Can you add a plant to my existing distillery?

Yes. CO₂ recovery, biogas, evaporation, spray drying and condensate polishing plants are regularly added to running distilleries, and skid-mounted sections keep site work to a minimum.

Do you work outside India?

Yes. We have references in more than 15 countries across Asia, Africa, the Americas and Oceania.

How do I start a project with MEGA?

Send your capacity, feedstock and site details to sales@mega.tc or call +91 72760 11520. Our engineers will review your requirement and prepare a proposal.
