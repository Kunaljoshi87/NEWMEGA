Critical Process Equipment

Need reliable process equipment? We engineer and fabricate critical equipment to code for petrochemical, chemical and ethanol plants.

Process engineering is the understanding and application of the fundamental principles and laws of nature that allow us to transform raw material and energy into products that are useful at an industrial level. We are working with wide Spectrum of Industrial Project for Distillery, Micro Brewery, Food, and Pharmaceutical & Process Industries.

Our exceptional Design ability and Project management enable us to fabricate and deliver wide range of custom made equipment as per Indian and International standards.

MEGA ENGINEERING is providing cutting edge technology to produce international quality with excellence in planning, engineering and project management. By considering all the standards and suitable MOC we provide equipment. We also export the only process equipment according to their standards.

Pressure Vessel:
• We design & manufacture all types of high quality pressure vessels from small to large heavy vessels.
• Our custom designed & fabricated vessels/ separators comply with international standards like ASME, TEMA etc.
• Design involves parameters such as maximum safe operating pressure and temperature, safety factor, corrosion allowance and minimum design temperature

Heat Exchangers:
• We manufacture majorly all types of heat exchangers as per TEMA class R, C and B Using HTRI.

Columns:
• We design, manufacture and supply customized process columns for a wide range of applications.
• Every chemical process industry work on columns so we understand and take responsibility of manufacturing with standards.
• We can design and Manufacture all types of conventional trays for any column size with various material of construction.
• We manufactures various types of trays like- Bubble Cap tray, Sieve Tray, Valve Tray etc.



Tank/Vessels:
• We design and fabricate storage tanks and processing vessels to your precise specifications for Chemical, Pharmaceutical, Distillery, Brewery, Food industries and many more process industries

Reactors :
• We design, manufacture and supply customized process Reactors for a wide range of applications. Every chemical manufacture company need reactor to carry out process reaction.
• Autoclave, jacketed non jacketed all type of reactor we manufacture as per client request.

Screw Conveyor :

• We are the designer and fabricator of screw converyor to different process manufaturing comapany according to their feasibility.
