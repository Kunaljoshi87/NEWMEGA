Engineering overview

MEGA provides design and fabrication of critical process equipment, including pressure vessels, heat exchangers, distillation columns, reactors and storage vessels. Mechanical design, material selection, fabrication and testing are aligned with the application and applicable project codes.

Capabilities & equipment

  • Pressure vessels and process separators
  • Shell-and-tube heat exchangers
  • Distillation and absorption columns
  • Trays, packing and column internals
  • Reactors, tanks and storage vessels
  • Fabrication, inspection and testing
