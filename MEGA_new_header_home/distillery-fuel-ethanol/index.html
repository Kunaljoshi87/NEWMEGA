Engineering Overview

MEGA supports distillery and bioethanol process engineering across feedstock preparation, fermentation, alcohol recovery, dehydration, process utilities and by-product management. Plant sections are integrated around feedstock quality and product requirements.

Capabilities & Equipment

  • Molasses, syrup and grain feedstock handling
  • Fermentation and yeast propagation
  • Multi-pressure distillation
  • Molecular sieve dehydration
  • Evaporation and by-product management
  • Utilities, automation and plant commissioning
