Engineering Overview

MEGA supports automation and control integration for process plants, including distillery, evaporation and water treatment applications. Design integrates measurement, control loops, interlocks, operator interfaces and plant-level operating requirements.

Capabilities & Equipment

  • Control philosophy and I/O definition
  • PLC, DCS and HMI integration
  • Temperature, pressure, flow and level measurement
  • Process interlocks and alarm management
  • Skid-to-plant controls integration
  • Commissioning and operator training

Frequently Asked Questions

Can controls be integrated with existing systems?

Skid and plant instrumentation can be coordinated with a client’s existing PLC or DCS subject to compatibility and interface definition.

What are typical engineering deliverables?

Control philosophy, instrument index, I/O list, functional descriptions, interlock matrix and commissioning documentation may be included as relevant.
