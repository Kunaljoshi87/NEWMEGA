Frequently asked questions

Can controls be integrated with existing systems?

Skid and plant instrumentation can be coordinated with a client’s existing PLC or DCS subject to compatibility and interface definition.

What are typical engineering deliverables?

Control philosophy, instrument index, I/O list, functional descriptions, interlock matrix and commissioning documentation may be included as relevant.
