FOR THE MEGA TEAM

How to Post a Blog Article (No Coding)

Anyone in the team with editor access can write and publish an article on the Insights page. You fill in a simple form, like writing an email.

Open the Blog Editor →

One-Time Setup (Ask the Website Admin)

  1. Create a free account on github.com with your work email.
  2. Send your GitHub username to the website admin. The admin adds you to the repository Mega-Engineering / NEWMEGA with Write access.
  3. Accept the invitation email from GitHub.

Write and Publish an Article

  1. Open the Blog Editor and click Sign in with GitHub.
  2. Choose Mega-Engineering / NEWMEGA, branch main.
  3. Click Blog articles (Insights), then Add an entry.
  4. Fill in: Article title, Article date, Author, Category and a Short summary (1–2 sentences).
  5. Upload a Featured image (landscape photo, at least 1200 px wide).
  6. Write the article in Article content. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, lists, links, images and tables.
  7. Turn on Publish on website and click Save.
  8. Wait about 2 minutes, then open the Insights page. Your article is there.
Save a draft: leave Publish on website off and click Save. Nobody sees it until you turn it on.

What the Website Does for You Automatically

  • Adds the article to the Insights page, the right category filter and the sitemap for Google.
  • Makes an "In this article" table of contents when you use 3 or more Heading 2 sections.
  • Shows the reading time, share buttons, a contact box and 3 related articles.
  • Uses the same header, footer and design as the rest of the website, on phone and computer.

Tips for a Good Article

  • Title: clear and specific, 50–70 characters.
  • Start with 2–3 sentences that say what the reader will learn.
  • Use Heading 2 for main sections and Heading 3 for sub-sections. Keep paragraphs short.
  • Use real MEGA photos. Do not use photos from the internet without permission.
  • Check numbers and names before you publish. End with a link to a MEGA page or sales@mega.tc.

Edit or Remove an Article

  • Edit: open the article in the Blog Editor, change it, click Save.
  • Hide: turn off Publish on website and Save.
  • Delete: use the Delete option in the editor (it cannot be undone).

Security: only people the admin adds to the repository can publish. No passwords are stored on this website.
