Cookie Policy

Learn how Mega Engineering uses cookies on this website, what each type does and how you can manage your cookie preferences.

What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small file made up of letters and numbers. Cookies store information on the browser of your computer or the hard drive of your mobile device.

How We Use Cookies?

We use cookies to distinguish you from other users of our website and to provide a personalized browsing experience. Cookies are used by Mega.tc so that our website can remember your actions while visiting, such as your log-in details, progress with inquiries, or items in your service requests.

What Type Of Cookies Do We Use?

Cookies can be session cookies or persistent cookies. Session cookies are deleted from your computer when you close your web browser. Persistent cookies remain on your computer until deleted or they reach their expiry date. We use the following cookies:

Analytical/Performance Cookies

These cookies help us recognise and count visitors and see how they navigate our website. This helps us improve our website’s performance, for example, by making it easier for users to find information.

Functionality Cookies

These cookies recognise you when you return to our website. This allows us to personalise content, greet you by name, remember your preferences, and support live chat during your browsing.

Targeting Cookies

These cookies track your visit, pages viewed, and links followed. We use this information to make our website and advertising more relevant to your interests. We may also share this data with third parties to customise advertisements and allow you to share or like content on other websites.

What Kind Of Information Do We Collect By Using Cookies?

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect the following types of information: your internet protocol (IP) address, login information, time zone, operating system and platform, details about your visits including the URL you came from, your country, search terms used on our website, services or products you viewed or searched, preferred locations, page response times, download errors, length of visits to certain pages, page interaction details (such as scrolling, clicks, and mouse-overs), and methods used to leave the page.
