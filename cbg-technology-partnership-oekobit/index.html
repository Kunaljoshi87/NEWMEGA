The Plant

From Organic Waste to Compressed Biogas

  1. Feedstock reception & preparationSolids feeders, shredders and liquid feeding; contaminant removal and hygienisation where the waste stream needs it.
  2. Anaerobic digestionInsulated digesters with heating circuits and agitators chosen for the substrate, running under continuous process control.
  3. Gas storage & cleaningDouble-membrane gas storage, desulphurisation and gas drying before upgrading.
  4. Upgrading & compressionCO₂ removal to biomethane quality, then compression to CBG for cascades, dispensing or pipeline.
  5. Digestate managementSolid–liquid separation and a plan for organic fertiliser, reuse or further treatment.

OEKOBIT Plant Technology

  • Substrate-flexible plant concepts
  • In-situ concrete digesters with wall insulation
  • Stainless-steel multi-circuit heating
  • Long-axis, paddle and central agitators
  • Double-membrane gas roofs
  • Integrated desulphurisation
  • Solids dosing with cutting rollers
  • Hygienisation for food and abattoir waste
  • Process automation and monitoring

Process route is indicative. Equipment selection and battery limits are fixed in each project proposal.

Gas Upgrading

Vehicle-Grade CBG, Engineered by MEGA

MEGA's MEGA-CBG upgrading system turns raw biogas (55–65% methane) into compressed biogas meeting IS 16087 for vehicle and industrial fuel.

  1. Raw biogas55–65% CH₄
  2. H₂S removalIron-chelate
  3. DryingMoisture removal
  4. CO₂ removal2-stage membrane
  5. Compression250 bar
  6. CBGIS 16087

Typical Performance

Product Methane≥ 97%
Product CO₂≤ 2%
Methane Recovery97–98%
Membrane Operating Pressure12–16 bar(g)
CBG Delivery Pressure250 bar
Typical Membrane Life5–10 years

Why It Works

  • No water, chemicals or regeneration heat for CO₂ removal
  • Stage-2 permeate recycled to cut methane slip
  • H₂S turned into recoverable elemental sulphur cake
  • Compact and modular: add modules to expand
  • PLC-automated, quick start-up and shutdown
  • FRP and SS316 construction against corrosion
