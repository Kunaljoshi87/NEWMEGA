Engineering overview

A modular process skid is a self-contained process system assembled within a transportable frame. MEGA combines process engineering, equipment fabrication, piping, structural design and electromechanical work to deliver compact systems built for integration at site.

Capabilities & equipment

  • 3D arrangement and maintainability planning
  • Integrated frames, vessels, piping and valves
  • Instrumentation, junction boxes and cabling
  • Solvent recovery, CIP and distillation skids
  • ATFD and water-treatment modules
  • Factory assembly and site hook-up planning

Frequently asked questions

What can be installed on a skid?

Typical applications include distillation, mixing, cleaning-in-place, solvent recovery, strippers, water treatment and evaporation modules.

What are the advantages?

Factory assembly can reduce site work, improve access to fabrication controls and help manage installation schedules.
