Engineering overview

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) combines effluent characterization, thermal concentration, solids management and condensate polishing to recover reusable water and manage the final residue. MEGA develops process-specific combinations of evaporators and dryers rather than a single fixed layout.

Capabilities & equipment

  • Effluent characterization and laboratory testing
  • Falling-film and forced-circulation evaporators
  • TVR/MVR and heat integration options
  • Agitated thin-film drying and solids handling
  • Condensate stripping, polishing and reuse
  • Plant energy and water balance integration

Frequently asked questions

What equipment is typically included in a ZLD plant?

A system can include pretreatment, multiple-effect evaporation, vapour recompression, concentrate drying and condensate polishing. The configuration depends on effluent properties.

How is condensate reused?

Recovered condensate can be treated through stripping, biological treatment, filtration and membranes as required by the target reuse quality.
