Engineering overview

MEGA engineers solvent recovery systems for chemical, pharmaceutical, food and other process industries. Solutions may use batch or continuous distillation, strippers, absorbers and packed or tray columns to separate valuable solvents and manage wastewater contaminants.

Capabilities & equipment

  • Batch and continuous solvent distillation
  • Steam stripping of volatile compounds
  • Packed and tray column design
  • Heat integration and utilities optimization
  • Condensers, receivers and solvent handling
  • Process controls and operational safety

Frequently asked questions

Can a recovery system run continuously?

Both batch and continuous configurations are available; selection depends on the solvent mixture, throughput and separation duty.

Which solvents can be considered?

The reference offering includes alcohols and several organic solvents. Final separation feasibility depends on composition, azeotropes and required purity.
