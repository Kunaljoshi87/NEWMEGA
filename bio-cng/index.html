Engineering overview

MEGA develops renewable gas systems around the characteristics of organic feedstocks and the intended gas use. Solutions can cover feedstock handling, anaerobic digestion, gas cleaning and upgrading, biomethane compression and digestate handling.

Capabilities & equipment

  • Feedstock characterization and preparation
  • Anaerobic digester engineering
  • Biogas collection and buffer storage
  • H₂S and moisture removal
  • CO₂ separation and methane upgrading
  • Compression, storage and digestate utilization

Frequently asked questions

What feedstocks can be used?

Suitable organic wastes and biomass depend on composition, moisture, contamination and achievable biogas yield; these are evaluated during process design.

What happens to digestate?

Digestate handling can include solid-liquid separation and treatment or nutrient recovery depending on the project configuration.
