Engineering overview

MEGA offers evaporation and drying systems for concentrating process streams, recovering condensate and converting liquid residues into manageable products. Technologies include falling-film, forced-circulation and agitated thin-film equipment with energy integration options.

Capabilities & equipment

  • Multiple-effect evaporation (MEE)
  • Falling-film and forced-circulation designs
  • Thermal and mechanical vapour recompression
  • Agitated thin-film dryers (ATFD)
  • Vinasse and spent-wash concentration
  • Condensate recovery and process integration

Frequently asked questions

How are evaporator types selected?

Selection depends on viscosity, boiling point elevation, fouling risk, solids content and energy balance.

Can evaporation be integrated with ZLD?

Yes. Evaporation and drying are core thermal concentration stages in many ZLD configurations.
